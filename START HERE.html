A good 5-minute tour

  1. Schedule: flip Normal week / Special weeks and browse a full finals or CAASPP week.
  2. Spirit: pick a spirit day, upload any outfit photo, hit Check my fit. Notice you can’t submit until the check passes.
  3. More tab: fill in a name, grade, and any 6-digit ID → Make my card. Tap the QR to blow it up.
  4. Teacher console: Load test student, type a custom amount, PIN 2627, give the bucks, watch the log.
  5. Try it on a phone width too. It’s built mobile-first.

Two honest caveats

Scanning the QR with an actual phone camera needs the app hosted at a URL (drag the FirebirdHub folder to Netlify Drop and it just works, teacher console included at /teacher.html). And the outfit check’s smart model + Google sign-in need internet; offline it falls back to a color check and says so. Feedback on anything: tell Abir. Go Firebirds.
