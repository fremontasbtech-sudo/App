How clubs work

Starting a new club

  1. Find a teacher advisor who can be at every meeting.
  2. Get 10+ interested students. All 10 type their own names from their own accounts so signatures can be verified.
  3. Write a club constitution in a Google Doc (purpose, membership requirements and obligations, officer roles) and share it with the clubs account.
  4. Submit the new-club application form, then complete your club interview.
  5. If you pass, you get a confirmation email from fremontclubs@gmail.com.

Missed this round? A second application round opens in January.

Staying official

  • Hold at least one meeting a month.
  • Complete the monthly check-in: a photo of your meeting with 10 members and your advisor.
  • Take part in at least 2 club events a year.
  • Keep your club’s Google Drive folder updated.

Strikes

Strike 1 is a warning, strike 2 is a meeting with you and your advisor, and strike 3 means the club is disbanded. You can appeal a strike within the same school year.
