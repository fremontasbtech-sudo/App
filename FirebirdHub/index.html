Start or run a club

Starting a new club

  1. Find a teacher advisor who can be at every meeting.
  2. Get 10+ interested students. All 10 type their own names from their own accounts so signatures can be verified.
  3. Write a club constitution in a Google Doc (purpose, membership requirements and obligations, officer roles) and share it with the clubs account.
  4. Submit the new-club application form, then complete your club interview.
  5. If you pass, you get a confirmation email from fremontclubs@gmail.com.

Missed this round? A second application round opens in January.

Staying official

  • Hold at least one meeting a month.
  • Complete the monthly check-in: a photo of your meeting with 10 members and your advisor.
  • Take part in at least 2 club events a year.
  • Keep your club’s Google Drive folder updated.

Strikes: strike 1 is a warning, strike 2 is a meeting with you and your advisor, and strike 3 means the club is disbanded. You can appeal a strike within the same school year.

      About this app

      Built by the ASB Tech Commission, ASB Leadership at Fremont High School, Sunnyvale (FUHSD). Spirit Wear submissions open only during spirit weeks. When they are open, they go only to ASB Spirit, Tech, and the advisor and are never shared publicly. Student IDs are used only to remove duplicate entries.

      Ask Felipe answers simple questions from the app's data on your device. Other questions, your last few messages and, when you ask about your assignments, the matching Schoology items (title, due date and short description) are sent to Google's Gemini AI to write an answer. Answers to general questions, like when the fall play is, are kept for up to 6 hours so other students get them faster, without your messages or assignments. ASB doesn't save your questions, and no one at ASB reads them. Please don't share private information with Felipe.
